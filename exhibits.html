How to Experience the Exhibition

Follow three simple principles to maximize your sensory exploration.

01

Calibrate your vision

Spend the first 5 minutes in the twilight welcome vestibule allowing your retinas to adjust to darkness for heightened color sensitivity.

02

Wander without screens

Pocket your phone and engage directly with the reactive surfaces. Touch the optical flora and observe your silhouette on light walls.

03

Rest in the sound bath

Conclude your journey in Aurora Frequency to ground your sensory system before transitioning back to the vibrant city outside.
