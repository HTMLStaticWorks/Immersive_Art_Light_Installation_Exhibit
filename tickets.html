Daily Timeslot Windows

Four distinct sensory atmospheres programmed throughout day and night.

Acoustic frequencies, ambient haze, and optical intensity dynamically shift across the day to harmonize with human biorhythms.

  • Morning Calm (10:00 AM – 12:30 PM): Lowest crowd capacity, gentle drone soundscapes, strobe-free sensory calm.
  • Afternoon Radiance (12:30 PM – 5:00 PM): Full-spectrum chromatic brilliance, highly active touch-reactive flora.
  • Sunset Twilight (5:00 PM – 7:30 PM): Meditative transition, rich indigo hues, prime photography conditions.
  • After-Dark Neon (7:30 PM – 11:00 PM): Kinetic laser matrixes, deep resonant bass sound baths, 18+ atmosphere.
