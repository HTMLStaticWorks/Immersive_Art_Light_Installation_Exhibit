# LUMINA — Immersive Art & Light Installation Exhibit

A premier immersive art and light installation website featuring 12 interactive chambers, volumetric laser architecture, and 360° spatial acoustics.

## Features
- **Home (index.html)**: Bold sensory layout with interactive sensory pathfinder, exhibit previews, and visitor testimonials.
- **Home 2 (home2.html)**: Full-bleed alternate sensory experience with interactive 5-phase visitor journey explorer.
- **Tickets & Timeslots (services.html / tickets.html)**: Timed-entry booking, VIP sensory passes, and timeslot guide.
- **Exhibits & Installations (counselors.html / exhibits.html)**: Showcase of 12 sensory zones, optical artist bios, and technical specifications.
- **Group & School Bookings (contact.html / groups.html)**: Educational STEM/Arts field trips, private corporate buyouts, and FAQs.
- **Sensory Journal (blog.html)**: Curatorial essays and optical physics research.
- **Visitor Dashboard (dashboard.html)**: Timed-entry ticket purchasing, date/slot selector, exhibit guide audio commentary, membership perks tracker, and payment receipts.
- **Visitor Portal (login.html & register.html)**: Account access and pass management.
- **Dark Mode & RTL Support**: Built-in chromatic dark/light mode toggle and RTL bidirectional layout support.
