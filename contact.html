Finding LUMINA

Exhibition Location & Interactive Map

Situated in the heart of the Arts & Innovation District. Seamlessly reachable via BART, Muni, and major expressways.

Public Transit & Rail

Powell St. & Montgomery St. BART/Muni stations are a 5-minute walk (0.3 mi). Muni bus lines 8, 14, 30, and 45 stop directly outside the pavilion doors.

Validated Parking

Validated underground parking is located at Fifth & Mission Garage (833 Mission St). All LUMINA ticket holders receive a $5 voucher discount at validation kiosks.

ADA Step-Free Entry

Dedicated step-free ground-level entrance with automatic sliding doors on Innovation Way. Motorcoach loading zone available for group arrivals.
